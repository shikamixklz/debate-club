THE CLUB

Come for the debate. Stay for the people.

The UMB Debate Club is a student community built around discussion, public speaking, critical thinking and curiosity.

WHO WE ARE

Different opinions make better conversations.

We bring students together to discuss questions that don't always have simple answers. Our meetings combine structured debates, open discussions and opportunities to practice communicating ideas.

You don't need to be an experienced debater. You just need to be willing to think, listen and occasionally defend an argument you didn't expect to make.

HOW A DEBATE WORKS

01

Choose the Topic

One debater proposes a topic they want to discuss and chooses a position: agree or disagree.

02

Make Your Case

The proposer explains their position and supports it with arguments, reasons and examples.

03

Challenge the Other Side

The opponent argues against the proposer's position, challenges their reasoning and presents a different perspective.

04

Vote for the Winner

After hearing both sides, participants use voting cards to choose the debater they believe made the stronger case.
